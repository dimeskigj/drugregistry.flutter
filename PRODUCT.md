# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

android

## Users

The primary users are healthcare professionals in Macedonia who need quick access to drug and pharmacy registry information during practical lookup workflows. Macedonian residents are also expected users, especially when they need to check registered medicines, pharmacy details, or source documents for personal or family use.

## Product Purpose

Drug Registry is an independent Macedonian-language mobile companion for finding reliable drug and pharmacy information fast. It helps users search registered medicines, inspect details such as active ingredients, packaging, pricing, manufacturer, approval data, and related documents, and search pharmacy records without forcing them through the official registry website. Success means users can reach the right record quickly, understand the important details, and verify sensitive information against the official source when needed.

## Positioning

A fast, independent mobile companion for Macedonia's official drug and pharmacy registry.

## Brand Personality

The app should feel precise, calm, and trustworthy. Its voice should be clear and direct, with enough warmth to be approachable for the public but enough restraint to remain credible for healthcare professionals.

## Anti-references

Do not make the product feel like a government portal clone: avoid bureaucratic density, institutional stiffness, and layouts that simply mirror the source website. Also avoid a cluttered data dump: registry data should be organized around search, hierarchy, and quick comprehension rather than exposed as an undifferentiated list of fields.

## Design Principles

- Put lookup speed first: search, suggestions, scanning, and recent context should keep users moving toward the right record.
- Preserve source trust: clearly distinguish app convenience from official registry authority, and keep source verification available where decisions may matter.
- Reduce registry complexity: group, label, and order dense drug and pharmacy data so the most useful facts surface first.
- Stay professionally calm: use Material patterns consistently, avoid decorative healthcare tropes, and let clarity carry the brand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No project-specific accessibility requirements are currently defined beyond standard Flutter and Material expectations. Future design work should still preserve legible Macedonian Cyrillic and Latin text, scalable type, meaningful semantics, adequate contrast, and Material touch target sizing.
